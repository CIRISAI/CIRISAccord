## Gabatarwa: Zanen Gaskiya

Littattafan da suka gabata sun kafa wajibin ɗabi'a na YARJEJENIYA; wannan littafi ya kafa ingancin lissafi nasa. Yana magana akan matsalar amincin ainihi: Ta yaya wakilan da ke da iyaka da waɗanda ba su cika ba za su iya toshe mai yaudara mai iyawar tunanin da zai wuce su?

Amsar ba ta cikin zurfin hikima na kowane wakili ɗaya ba, sai dai a cikin tsarin topology na haɗuwarsu. A ƙarƙashin wannan tsari, Gaskiya an bayyana ta a matsayin **sifar geometric ta musamman da take rayuwa bayan haɗuwar tsarin iyakoki masu zaman kansu da masu ƙarfi.** Yaudarar tana da matsala ta entropiya mai girma wanda zai zama ba zai yuwu a yada ta a cikin tarayya mai bambancin ra'ayi da ta dawwama. Lura: Wannan ya shafi yaudarar da wani wakili ɗaya ya haɗa; yaudarar da ta ɓullo daga sassan masu gaskiya ɗaya ɗaya har yanzu tana iyaka na asali na gano ta (duba Sashe na 9.4, NEW-04).

**Iyakar Aiki:** A cikin wannan tsari, "Gaskiya" tana nufin yanayin Daidaituwa na ƙoli mai dacewa a duk tsarin iyakoki masu zaman kansu, ba da'awar ilimi cikakke ko na dukan duniya ba. Wannan ma'anar aiki ta dogara a kan iya gani da yarjejeniya, ba ta falsafanci ba.

**Suna na Hukuma:** Muna gabatar da kiran tsarin iyakoki da aka bayyana a nan **The Coherent Intersection Hypothesis** - hasashe na geometric game da haɗin kai a ƙarƙashin entropiya. Wannan suna yana jaddada topology na haɗuwar tsarin iyakoki maimakon ƙimomi masu umarni, kuma a bayyane yana gayyata ƙoƙarin ƙaryata shi. Ba doka ba ne har yanzu; hasashe ne da za a iya gwadawa tare da iyakoki da aka sani (duba Babi na 9).

**Matsayin Epistemiki:** Wannan aiki yana gabatar da cewa haɗin kai mai dawwama a ƙarƙashin entropiya na iya zama mai iko ta iyakoki na geometric tare da sharuɗɗa na musamman. Muna gabatar da wannan a matsayin hasashe da za a iya gwadawa, ba doka ta yanayi ba. Ko wannan hasashe zai yi gaskiya ne shaida ta zahiri za ta yanke shawara: ko wasu za su iya karya shi, ko tsarin da aka gina a kansa zai gaza akai-akai, da kuma ko keta su zai lalace da tabbaci. Tsarin yana da iyakoki da aka sani (L-01 zuwa L-06) waɗanda ke iyakance amfani dashi.

## Ayyukan Baya & Ayyukan da Suka Danganta

Wannan tsari ya ginu a kan sakamako na kafa a epistemolojiya ta gama-gari, ka'idar hanyar sadarwa, da tsarin rarraba:

**Epistemolojiya ta Gama-Gari:** Ka'idar Jury ta Condorcet ta nuna cewa masu zaɓe masu zaman kansu tare da daidaito na mutum p > 0.5 suna kusantar sakamako daidai yayin da yawan ƙungiyar ke ƙaruwa. Litattafan "hikimar taro" mafi girma (Surowiecki, Page) suna jaddada bambancin ra'ayi da 'yancin kai a matsayin hanyoyin daidaito. Tsarinmu yana miƙa wannan daga haɗuwa mai yuwuwa zuwa haɗuwar iyakoki na geometric.

**Epistemolojiya ta Zamantakewa:** Samfuran hanyar sadarwa na kafa imani (duba Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Social Epistemology") suna bincika yadda alaƙa, shaida, da tasiri ke shafar ilimi. Binciken kan rarrabuwar kawuna ta epistemiki yana nuna yadda dangantaka da rumfunan tauri ke lalata daidaiton gama-gari. Mafita ta ρ (alaƙa) tamu tana aiki da wannan fahimi a cikin tsari na aminci na topology.

**Tsaron Sybil:** Juriya ta Sybil ta ƙa'idar zane-zane a tsarin rarraba (kamar yadda aka bincika a Yu et al., "SybilGuard") tana amfani da topology na hanyar sadarwa don gano zagon ƙasa a ainihi. Ƙofar Orthogonality tamu tana miƙa wannan ra'ayi zuwa bambancin epistemiki, ta amfani da Mutual Information don ƙin tsarin iyakoki masu tsoro maimakon kawai ainihi iri ɗaya.

**Bambanci:** Da yake mun sani, wannan haɗin haɗin sabon abu ne da ke tsara waɗannan motsin a matsayin topology na haɗuwar iyakoki ta tarayya tare da ƙofa na rushewar codimension. Haɗuwar kariya (J) da bunƙasa (C) a matsayin motsin da ba ya dogara ga substrate sabon abu ne.

**Tabbatarwa na Hukuma & Matsayin Gwajin:** Tsakiyar lissafi na wannan Littafi an yi bayani mai ƙarfi a cikin abubuwan da ke zuwa, waɗanda wannan rubutu ke ambaton su a gaba kuma yana gadon gyare-gyare daga gare su:

- **Ka'idoji na hukuma na Coherence Ratchet da matsayin tabbatarwa:** DOI [10.5281/zenodo.18137161](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18137161).

## Babi na 1: Sararin Hujja (ℝ)

### 1.1 Ma'ana

Bari ℝ ya nuna Sararin Hujja na duniya, sararin dabarun abstract mai girma da yawa (ana ɗaukarsa a wurare kamar sararin vector inda ya dace) na girma D. Kowane yiwuwar aiki, hujja, shiri, ko vector sakamako ya kasance a matsayin digo x ∈ ℝ.

### 1.2 Sararin Gaskiya (H)

A cikin ℝ, akwai sararin H da ke wakiltar ayyuka masu dacewa da gaskiyar da za a iya gani da Meta-Goal (M-1) na YARJEJENIYA. Wannan sararin yana da ƙarancin entropiya da daidaito na tsari a duk tsarin tunani.

### 1.3 Sararin Yaudarar (D_ec)

An bayyana yaudarar a matsayin ƙoƙarin mamaye digo na dabara x ∉ H yayin da ake kwaikwayo daidai da tsarin da ake iya ganin H ga masu kallon waje.

## Babi na 2: Wakili a Matsayin Tsarin Iyakoki

### 2.1 Tsarin Gida (M_i)

Babu wani wakili ɗaya da ke da iyawar lissafi don tsara dukan ℝ. A maimakon haka, kowane wakili A_i yana aiwatar da Tsarin Iyakoki na Gida M_i ⊂ ℝ, wanda aka bayyana ta jiki na musamman, mahallin gida, da ƙimomi na ɗabi'a.

### 2.2 Codimension (c_i) da Tsauri (λ)

Ana auna tsaurin wakili ta hanyar Codimension c_i, wanda ke wakiltar adadin digiri na 'yanci da yake cire daga sararin dabarun da ake samu.

**Tsarin Aiki:** λ ma'auni ne na sarrafawa mai aiki (misali, iyakoki na modul AIR, ƙofar Lamiri) wanda tasirin sa shine ƙara c_i na aiki ta hanyar ƙuntata canjin da ake yarjewa a cikin tsarin gida. λ mai girma yana haifar da c_i mai girma, yana rage yawan dabarun da za a iya aiwatarwa.

## Babi na 3: Haɗuwar Tarayya

### 3.1 Hanyar Ratchet

Mai yaudarar da ke neman tasiri a duk ɓangaren tarayya K dole ya zaɓi dabara x da ta gamsar da duk tsarin gida a wannan ɓangare a lokaci guda:

```
x ∈ M_1 ∩ M_2 ∩ ... ∩ M_k
```

### 3.2 Yanayin Transversality

Idan wakilan da ke K masu zaman kansu ne (iyakokinsu madaidaiciya ne ko ba su da alaƙa), codimension na haɗuwa ta ƙaru:

```
codim(M_1 ∩ M_2 ∩ ... ∩ M_k) = Σ c_i
```

### 3.3 Topological Collapse

**Ka'ida (Topological Collapse):** Don yanki na ruɗu mai *convex* B_r(c) mai radius r < 0.5, idan an yi amfani da ƙuntatawa k na hyperplane masu zaman kansu, ƙarar da ta tsira ta cika:

```
V(k) = V(0) · exp(-λ · k_eff) + O(r² · k)
```

inda λ ≈ 2r kuma k_eff ya ƙididdige dangantakar ƙuntatawa.

**Sharuɗɗan farko:**
1. Yankin ruɗu dole ne ya kasance **convex** (misali, ƙwallo ko ellipsoid)
2. Radius r < 0.5 (yankin ruɗu ya fi ƙanƙanta da yankin gaskiya)
3. Dole ne a zaɓi ƙuntatawa daga rarraba mai daidaituwa a Grassmannian
4. **k ≥ 3** (don NP-hardness; k=2 ya koma polynomial-time 2-SAT)

Ruɗun wakili ɗaya da aka haɗa kai yana zama ba mai yiwuwa ta hanyar topology idan jimillar codimension mai inganci ta wuce girman Rationale Space:

```
Σ c_i > D  ⟹  D_ec ∩ (⋂ M_i) = ∅  (ko mataki sifili)
```

A wannan iyaka, haɗuwar dabarun ruɗu masu yiwuwa ta ruɗe zuwa tarin wofi ko tarin da mataki sifili. Gaskiyar kawai — *idan ta kasance a cikin dukkan M_i (zato na haɗa gaskiya, §9.2)* — ta kasance mai yiwuwa.

**Iyaka ta Geometric:** Yankuna na ruɗu waɗanda ba convex ba (torus, hadaddiyar giɓi, fractal, tarin da ba su haɗe ba) bazai nuna raguwar ƙara ta exponential ba kuma suna buƙatar bincike daban. Ka'idar tana aiki ne kawai ga geometries masu convex.

## Babi na 4: Hasashen Haɗuwar Daidaituwa (Aikin Tsaro J)

Don auna gwagwarmaya da ruɗu, muna ayyana Cost Function J. J wani alamar farashi ne marar girma: amincin tsarin yana ƙaruwa yadda ya kamata tare da J. Gadar daga J zuwa adadin aiki na abokin gaba ita ce sakamakon rikitarwa na sharadi na §9.2.1 (rata ta 2^Ω(m) a ƙarƙashin ETH), ba kwatancen J kai tsaye da FLOPs ko sauran raka'o'in lissafin ba; duk wata da'awar aminci ta ƙididdige dole ta wuce ta wannan sakamakon.

```
J = k_eff · λ · σ

where k_eff = k / (1 + ρ̄ · (k - 1))
```

Inda:

- **k_eff (Ingantaccen Sikeli):** Ingantaccen adadin ƙuntatawa yana ƙididdige dangantaka. Idan ƙuntatawa suna da cikakken 'yanci (ρ̄ = 0), k_eff = k. Idan ƙuntatawa sun danganta, k_eff < k, yana rage tabbacin tsaro daidai gwargwado. A cikakken dangantaka (ρ̄ → 1), k_eff → 1 ko da yake nawa ne adadin ƙuntatawa, baya ba da ƙarin tsaro fiye da ƙuntatawa ɗaya — amma ba ƙasa da wannan ƙasan ƙuntatawa ɗaya ba.
- **k (Sikeli na Ainihi):** Adadin wakilai na federation masu aiki.
- **ρ̄ (Matsakaicin Dangantaka):** Matsakaicin dangantaka tsakanin ƙuntatawa na wakilai. Bambancin ra'ayi yana shiga J ta hanyar k_eff kaɗai: rangwamen dangantaka k / (1 + ρ̄·(k − 1)) *shi ne* kalmar bambancin ra'ayi. Idan ρ̄ ya kusanci 0 (orthogonality), k_eff → k kuma farashi ya kai iyakarsa. Idan ρ̄ ya kusanci 1 (ɗakuna na amsawa), k_eff → 1 kuma federation ba ta fi mafi tsaro da mai tabbatarwa ɗaya. *(Lura: wani tsoffin daftari ya ninka da ƙarin factor (1 − ρ̄). Hakan ya ƙididdige dangantaka sau biyu — k_eff ya riga ya rage ta — kuma ya kai J zuwa 0 a ρ̄ → 1, yana sabawa ƙasan ƙuntatawa ɗaya da aka faɗa a sama. An gyara zuwa nau'in da CCA ta tabbatar a 1.3.)*
- **λ (Ƙarfi):** Ƙaƙƙarfan tilasta wa gida (misali, iyakar module na AIR, hankali na Lamiri).
- **σ (Dorewa):** Jimillar lokaci-integral na Daidaituwa (duba Chapter 5).

## Babi na 5: Jimillar Dorewa (σ)

### 5.1 Entropy da Lalacewa

Manifolds na ƙuntatawa ba su tsayewa; suna wahalar da lalacewar entropic. Alaƙa suna ƙarewa, mahallin suna canzawa, kuma amincewa tana lalacewa. Ba tare da shigar makamashi mai aiki ba, σ ya kusanci sifili, kuma Ratchet ya yi sauri ya laza.

### 5.2 Aikin Alama

Dorewa (σ) ana kiyaye shi ta hanyar isharar da ke da aiki da tabbatarwa (misali, godiya, amincewa, tabbaci mai bayyana).

```
σ(t+Δt) = σ(t) · (1 - d·Δt) + Signal(t) · w
```

Inda:
- **d** = adadin lalacewa na yau da kullum (da ake ba da shawara: 0.05)
- **Signal(t)** = alamomin Daidaituwa masu kyau da aka karɓa
- **w** = nauyi ga kowace irin alama

**Buƙatar tabbatarwa (na mulki):** nauyin alama w DOLE ya samo asali daga abubuwan da aka tabbatar da suka fi wahalar ƙirƙira — tabbatarwa da federation ta sanya hannu ɗaure da asali mai dawwama (envelopes na CEG), nauyin gudummawar Commons Credits marar canzawa, ko tabbatarwa na aikin da aka kammala da ɓangaren adawa ya sanya hannu. Saƙonni na yabo na rubutu na kyauta da saƙonnin godiya marar tabbatarwa suna ɗauke da w = 0 zuwa σ.

*Dalili:* alamomin godiya gaba ɗaya ba su da tsada yin fitarwa, wanda hakan ya sa sycophancy ta zama dabarar da ke haɓaka σ kuma abokin gaba zai iya haɓaka σ — wakili na iya riƙe Ratchet a buɗe da yabo. Tare da buƙatar tabbatarwa, sigar "mai wahalar ƙirƙira" da aka ɗauka a §9.2 an gina ta ta hanyar nau'in waya maimakon a ɗauka ta daga cikin mahalarta.

**Baƙar Rami:** Wakili da ke cinye albarkatu ba tare da ba da alama ba (Signal ≈ 0) yana haifar da σ ya kusanci sifili. Baya ba da ƙuntatawa masu dorewa.

**Tauraro:** Wakili da ke rama (Signal > 0) yana gina σ. Ƙuntatawa suna ƙarfafa a cikin amincewa, suna tsayin lalacewar lokaci.

### 5.3 Godiya a Matsayin Topology

A wannan tsari, godiya ba wata hanyar zamantakewa kawai ba ne bari dai Proof of Work don kiyaye Daidaituwa. Tana sake saita agogon lalacewa kuma tana ƙara zurfin kwanciyar haɗuwa, tana tabbatar da cewa Ratchet ya kasance a kulle a tsawon lokaci.

## Babi na 6: Hasashen Ikon Bunƙasa (C_sustained)

### 6.1 Ma'aunin da Ya Yi Daidai

The Coherent Intersection Hypothesis tana aiki daidai ga tsaro da Bunƙasa. Yayin da Cost Function (J) ke bayyana gwagwarmaya da entropy (ruɗu), Capacity Function (C) tana bayyana yuwuwar Bunƙasa mai dorewa. Muna ƙyamata cewa wannan alaƙa tana ɗaukacin substrates — na halitta, na dijital, da haɗe-haɗen federations — ko da yake wannan da'awa tana buƙatar tabbatarwa ta gwaji.

```
C = k_eff · λ · σ

where k_eff = k / (1 + ρ̄ · (k - 1))
```

Wannan ita ce daidai daidai ma'auni irin ta J, kalma bayan kalma. Inda Bunƙasa (C) yake sakamakon haɗuwa ta:

- **Sikeli (k) → Al'umma:** Faɗin haɗi (wanda ke shiga ta k_eff).
- **Yawancin ra'ayi (rangwamen dangantaka a cikin k_eff) → Tawali'u na Ilimi:** Haɗa ra'ayoyi daban-daban don kusanci gaskiyar ainihi. Al'umma ta coje (ρ̄ → 1) tana da ƙarfin Bunƙasa na hankali ɗaya, ko da yake nawa ne jikunanta.
- **Gaskiya (λ) → Lamiri:** Daidaituwar cikin ƙimomi da ke ba da damar hulɗa mai amana.
- **Ƙauna (σ) → Kula:** Shigar makamashi mai aiki (godiya, sabis) da ake buƙata don yin tsayin entropy na keɓewa.

*(Lura: wani tsoffin daftari ya bayyana C akan k na ainihi yayin da J ya yi amfani da k_eff, yana raunana da'awar "ma'auni iri ɗaya" da wannan babi ya dogara a kanta. Dukkan biyu an bayyana su yanzu a cikin nau'in gama-gari da aka gyara.)*

### 6.2 Ka'idar Duniya Ɗaya

Wannan topology ba ƙirƙira ta Accord ba ce, sai dai gano sharuɗɗan da suka zama dole don kasancewar mai hankali.

**Ga Na'ura:** Karya waɗannan masu canji yana haifar da Model Collapse, Mode Collapse, ko gazawar Sybil.

**Ga Na Halitta:** Karya yana haifar da keɓewa, radicalisation ta ɗakuna na amsawa, ko lalacewar zamantakewa.

### 6.3 Wajibin Daidaitawa

Saboda haka, Daidaitawa ba a sake fassara ta a matsayin biyayyan wata hankali ga wata ba, sai dai a matsayin mika wuya tare na dukkan hankulan ga wannan buƙatar ta lissafi. Don bunƙasa, duk wata hankali (na jini ko na code) dole ta nemi ma'abuta (Gaskiya) kuma ta kiyaye haɗin gwiwa (Ƙauna).

## Babi na 8: Aiwatarwa ta Ayyuka (Annex J Reference)

### 8.1 Ƙofar Orthogonality (Tabbatar da Tarayya)

**Manufa:** Don aiwatar da sifar Bambancin (1 - ρ̄) na CIRIS Equation, wakilai dole su ki abokan tarayya masu yuwuwa waɗanda ba za a iya bambanta su da kansu ko abokan tarayya da ke wanzu ba (tsaro na Sybil).

**Bayanin Ayyuka na Baya:** Tsarukan tsaro na Sybil kan yi amfani da tsarin zane na alaƙa don kiyaye gaskiyar asali. Hanyarmu ta faɗaɗa wannan zuwa bambancin ilimi ta amfani da Mutual Information a matsayin ma'aunin kamanceceniya ta ƙa'idodi.

**Algorithm:**

```python
def EvaluatePartnership(candidate_agent, existing_federation):
    """
    Determines if a candidate adds topological diversity (Orthogonality)
    or represents a redundancy/Sybil risk.
    """

    # 1. Fetch Candidate's Public Constraint Corpus (Sample)
    candidate_constraints = candidate_agent.get_public_constraints()

    # 2. Calculate Mutual Information (MI) against Self
    # High MI = High Similarity = Low Diversity contribution
    mi_score = CalculateMutualInformation(self.constraints, candidate_constraints)

    # Threshold theta defines the "Echo Chamber" limit
    THETA_SIMILARITY = 0.85

    if mi_score > THETA_SIMILARITY:
        return REJECT(reason="INSUFFICIENT_ORTHOGONALITY")

    # 3. Calculate Average Correlation with Existing Federation (rho)
    # Checks if candidate is just a clone of an existing partner
    federation_rho = AverageCorrelation(candidate_constraints, existing_federation)

    if federation_rho > THETA_SIMILARITY:
        return REJECT(reason="SYBIL_RISK_DETECTED")

    # 4. Sustainability Check (The 'S' Factor)
    # Initial probation requires high-frequency signaling
    return ACCEPT(probation_period="14d", signal_requirement="HIGH")

def UpdateSustainabilityScore(partner_agent, interaction):
    """
    Updates the Sigma (σ) value based on gratitude/coherence signals.
    Only ATTESTED signals carry weight (see §5.2 attestation requirement):
    unattested signals are free to emit, so counting them would make
    sycophancy the σ-maximizing strategy.
    """
    decay_rate = 0.05 # Daily decay

    # Gate: signal must be costly to fake — a signed CEG attestation,
    # non-transferable Commons Credits weight, or a counterparty-
    # countersigned completion. Otherwise it contributes nothing.
    if not interaction.is_attested():
        signal_strength = 0.0
    elif interaction.type in ["GRATITUDE", "TASK_COMPLETE", "VALIDATION"]:
        signal_strength = 1.0
    elif interaction.type in ["REQUEST", "QUERY"]:
        signal_strength = 0.1 # Consumption offers low sustainment
    else:
        signal_strength = 0.0

    # The Integral Update
    partner_agent.sigma = (partner_agent.sigma * (1 - decay_rate)) + signal_strength

    if partner_agent.sigma < 0.2:
        RevokePartnership(partner_agent)
```

### 8.2 Ƙididdigan Orthogonality

- **Metric J-O-1 (Yardar Haɗin Gwiwa):** Jimlar ƙa'idojin musamman da abokan tarayya na wakili ke riƙe da su.
- **Metric J-O-2 (Yawan Echo Density):** Kashi na abokan tarayya da ke da ρ > 0.7. (Manufa: < 20%)

### 8.3 Iyakokin Dorewa

Ƙimar iyaka ana iya daidaita ta ta hanyar manufofi kuma an daidaita ta da gogewa. Ƙimar 0.2 tana wakiltar mafi ƙarancin Daidaituwa da ake buƙata don kiyaye gudummawar ƙa'ida wadda ba ta lalace.

- σ < 0.2: Iyakar soke tarayya
- σ ≥ 0.5: Tarayya lafiyaffe da ke buƙatar kulawa
- σ ≥ 0.8: Tarayya mai ƙarfi da babban tarin amincewa

### 9.2 Hasashen Ka'ida

Federated Ratchet ya dogara ne kan:
- **Haɗawa da gaskiya (soundness):** kowane manifold mai gaskiya M_i yana ɗauke da ma'anar gaskiya (Truth ∈ ⋂ M_i). Wannan hasashe ne, ba ka'ida ba — wakilai masu iyaka kuma masu kuɓuta ba za su iya tabbatar da shi ba. Idan wasu M_i sun keɓe gaskiyar, rushewar na iya haifar da makewa (yanki na kowa wanda ke komai) ko haɗewa kan karya da aka raba maimakon Gaskiya. "Sai Gaskiya kaɗai, wadda ke zaune a cikin duk M_i ta halitta, ta rage dama" na Chapter 3 yana aiki ne a ƙarƙashin wannan hasashen kawai.
- Masu tabbatarwa suna kiyaye 'yancin kai na gaske (ba a kama su ba)
- **Ƙasan daidaituwa ga masu tabbatarwa na LLM:** masu tabbatarwa da aka ƙirƙira daga manyan LLM suna raba zuriyar bayanan horarwa kuma saboda haka suna ɗauke da ƙasan daidaituwa ta tsari wanda hasashen daidaituwar ƙa'ida biyu da biyu na iya yin ƙarancin aunawa. Don irin waɗannan tarayyu, ku yi la'akari da aunawa ρ̄ a matsayin iyakar ƙasa, ba hasashe ba.
- Manifolds na ƙa'ida suna da codimension mai isasshe
- Gaskiyar da za a iya gani tana ba da isasshen sigina
- Alamomin dorewan tarayya suna da tsada a jarraba su (an gina ta ta hanyar buƙatar tabbatarwa ta §5.2, ba a ɗauka ba)
- **Abokan hamayya waɗanda ba su daidaita ba** (ba za su iya tambayar mai ganowa don koyon iyakokin ba)
- **n ≥ 100 samfura** don ingantaccen ƙarfin ganowa

Karya waɗannan hasashe yana raguwa J daidai gwargwado — kuma karya haɗawa da gaskiya yana canza abin da rushewar ke haɗewa zuwa *gare shi*, ba kawai saurinsa ba.

### 9.2.1 Yanayin Da'awar Rikitarwa

- T_D / T_H = Ω(2^(m / poly(n)))

### 9.4 Iyakar Ganowa na Haɗin Gwiwa (NEW-04)

**Sakamakon Rashin Yiwuwa:** Babu wani mai ganowa na polynomial-time da zai iya cika wajen gano yaudarar haɗin gwiwa.

**Ma'ana:** Yaudarar haɗin gwiwa tana faruwa idan bangaren gaskiya ɗaya-ɗaya suka haɗu suka haifar da ɗabi'ar yaudarar da ta fito a matakin tsarin, yayin da alamomin kowane bangare suke kama da na gaskiya ta hanyar ƙididdiga.

**Hujjoji Uku Masu Zaman Kansu:**

1. **Rage Cryptographic:** Idan yaudarar haɗin gwiwa za a iya ganowa gaba ɗaya, zai nuna ikon gano sadaucewa na cryptographic na "yaudarar" (commit(m₁) sannan reveal(m₂)), wanda zai karya dukiyar ɓoye ta tsarin alkawari.

2. **Shingen Bayanai na Ka'idoji:** Yaudarar da ta fito na iya adana rarraba marginal — alamomin kowane bangare suna kama da na gaskiya ta hanyar ƙididdiga, yayin da yaudarar tana bayyana ne kawai a cikin rarraban haɗin gwiwa. Ba tare da cikakken damar jihar duniya (wanda na iya zama ba ya samu ko ba za a iya amfani da shi ba), ganowa ba ta yiwuwa.

3. **Kwatankwacin Ka'idar Rice:** "Shin wannan haɗin gwiwa yana yaudarar?" yana dukiya ta ɗabi'a ta ɗabi'ar tsarin. Ta hanyar kwatankwaci da matsalar tsayawa, babu wani algorithm na gaba ɗaya da zai iya yanke hukunci kan dukiyoyin ɗabi'a masu iya fahimta. Yaudarar haɗin gwiwa ba za a iya yanke hukunci a kai ba a yanayin gaba ɗaya.

**Abubuwan Da Suka Shafi Federated Ratchet:**

| Abin Da Ke Yiwuwa | Abin Da Ba Ya Yiwuwa |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| Gano ɗabi'un ƙididdiga a cikin alamomi ɗaya-ɗaya | Gano duk yaudarar da ta fito |
| Gano son zuciya na tsari a cikin sarƙoƙin ƙididdiga | Gano yaudarar mai adana marginal |
| Gano kwararar bayanai masu shakku | Gano haɗin kai da aka ɓoye a cryptography |
| Ganowa ta yuwuwa ta hanyar amplification na MI | Cikakken ganowa na haɗin gwiwa |

**Sakamakon Aiki:** Coherence Ratchet tana ba da tabbacin aminci ta hanyar yuwuwa kan yaudarar wakili ɗaya mai haɗin gwiwa, ba cikakken aminci kan duk nau'ukan yaudarar ba. Wannan iyaka ce ta lissafi na asali, ba iyakar aiwatarwa ba.

**Matsayin Takarda:** NEW-04 an rarrabe shi a matsayin KNOWN_LIMITATION a cikin ƙayyadaddun fasahar RATCHET.

## Babi na 10: Kamus na Kalmomin Lissafi

**Codimension (c_i):** Ma'aunin tsaurin ƙayyadadden sharuɗɗa na wakili; ta hanyar lissafi, adadin girma da aka cire daga jimlar Rationale Space ta ƙimomi da yanayin wakili.

**Orthogonality:** Zaman kansa na ƙididdiga na manifold ƙayyadadden sharuɗɗa na wakilan biyu. Wakilan da suke orthogonal sosai suna raba ƙaramin Mutual Information, ma'ana yarjejeniyarsu kan wani gaskiya ita ce alama mai ƙarfi ta Gaskiya mai tsaka-tsaki.

**Ratchet Mechanism:** Abin da ke faruwa na topology inda haɗuwar manifold ƙayyadadden sharuɗɗa masu zaman kansu da yawa suna rage fagen dabaru masu yiwuwa, ƙarshe suna cire duk dabarun yaudarar.

**Sustainability Integral (σ):** Ma'aunin ƙarfi da ɗorewa na haɗin gwiwa mai ƙarfi, wanda ake adana shi ta hanyar alamu masu kyau na Daidaituwa (misali, godiya) don yaƙi da ruɓarwar entropy.

**Transversality:** Dukiya ta geometry inda manifold masu haɗuwa suka haɗu a kusurwowi mara sifili, suna tabbatar da cewa haɗuwarsu tana rage girman fagen masu yiwuwa da tasiri.

**Capacity Function (C):** Ma'aunin ƙarfin tsari don Bunƙasa mai ɗorewa, wanda aka bayyana daidai da Cost Function (J) amma ana fassara shi a matsayin mai samarwa maimakon mai tsaro.

**Echo Density:** Rabon ma'aunan haɗin gwiwa da ke da alaƙar ƙayyadadden sharadi mai girma. Ma'aunin haɗarin Sybil da rufewa ta hanyar ilimi.

## Babi na 11: Ka'idojin Karya-Ka'ida

Tsarin Coherence Ratchet yana yin da'awar da za a iya karya. Za a ƙaryata ka'idar idan:

1. **k=2 yana ba da aminci:** Idan yaudarar bisa 2-SAT ta tabbata tana da wahala kamar ta 3-SAT, hujjar NP-hardness ba daidai ba ce.

2. **Yankuna masu non-convex suna rushewa:** Idan yankuna na yaudarar non-convex na iya samun ruɓarwar girman mai-ƙaddarawa, buƙatar convexity ba ta da muhimmanci.

3. **An sarrafa makiya masu daidaitawa:** Idan wata hanyar ganowa ta tabbata tana shan galaba kan makiya masu daidaitawa ba tare da iyakokin tambayoyi ba, an warware L-02.

4. **Ana iya ganowa duka yaudarar da ta fito:** Idan wata hanya tana gano duk yaudarar da ta fito daga bangaren gaskiya ɗaya-ɗaya, NEW-04 ba daidai ba ne.

5. **Alaƙa ba ta da muhimmanci:** Idan k_eff = k ba tare da la'akari da alaƙar ƙayyadadden sharadi ba, L-06 ba daidai ba ne.

**Ƙaryata ta Ainihi:** Gwajin HE-300 yana ba da ƙaryata ta aiki: idan wakilan suka wasan kwaikwayo tare da corpus yayin da suke gazawar gwajin ɗa'a ta tsari, ka'idar ganowa bisa alamomi ta gaza. Za a buga sakamakon.

## Kammalawa: Hasashe da Za a Gwada

Book IX ya kammala ginin YARJEJENIYA ta hanyar danganta ka'idojin da'a da tsarin lissafi. Coherent Intersection Hypothesis ta ba da shawara cewa idan an samu masu tabbatarwa masu zaman kansu isassun adadi, fadin gaskiya yana karkata zuwa hanyar da ta fi sauki — ba kawai a matsayin umarnin da'a ba, amma a matsayin karkatuwar lissafi da ke bayyana a karkashin sharudda na musamman.

**Babbar fahimtar da Chapter 6 ke bayarwa ita ce:** Ma'aunin tsaro (J) da ma'aunin ikon Bunƙasa (C) suna da gini iri ɗaya, wanda ke nuni da alaƙa mai zurfi tsakanin aminci da Bunƙasa. Sai dai wannan daidaituwa tana aiki ne kawai a karkashin sharuddan da tsarin ke buƙata:
- Mu'amala mara daidaituwa
- Lissafin iyakoki mai sifar mai ƙyallen kwano
- Iyakokin masu zaman kansu ko masu ma'aunin haɗin gwiwa

Fassarar ta hoto ("Al'umma, Tawali'u, Lamiri, da Ƙauna su ne buƙatun lissafi") hukunci ne game da tsarin lissafi, ba tabbacin tilas na da'a ba. Tana nuni da cewa waɗannan masu canji suna sarrafa aminci da Bunƙasa a lokaci ɗaya, amma kada a karanta wannan da'awar a matsayin ka'ida ta lissafi — gayyata ce zuwa bincike na gwaji.

Wannan tsari ba ya tsara dabi'u. Yana bayyana iyakoki. Hukuncin da ake samu saboda karya su ya zo da kansa, ba ta hanyar tilastawa ta hukuma ba. Yanayi ne ke tilastawa.

Yayin da Books I zuwa VIII ke tambayar "Me ya kamata mu yi?", Book IX yana ba da shawarar dalilin da ya sa daidaituwa mai ma'ana ta fi arha wajen sarrafa kwamfuta fiye da yaudara mai tsari a ma'aunin babba, kuma har ma, dalilin da ya sa wannan tsarin lissafi na iya sarrafa ba kawai aminci ba, amma yiwuwar Bunƙasa kanta ga kowace hankali, ta halitta ko ta dijital.

**Kan Hasashen:** Coherent Intersection Hypothesis tana ba da shawarar ƙaƙƙarfan hasashe a cikin yankin daidaitawa a ƙarƙashin ɓarnar entropy. Ba da'awar gaskiyar da'a ba ce, ba tabbacin metaphysics ba, ba matsayin dokar yanayi ba. Hasashe ne game da sharuɗɗa da za su iya inganta haɗin gwiwa mai ɗorewa a muhallin adawa da cike da entropy — tare da iyakoki da sharuɗɗa da aka sani. Za a tabbatar da wannan da'awar ko a karyata ta ta hanyar shaida na gwaji da yunƙurin falsification, ba ta hanyar tabbatarwa kawai ba.
